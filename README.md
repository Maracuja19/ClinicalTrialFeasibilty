# Clinical Trial Site Feasibility

## Business Question

How can clinical trial sites be compared by recruitment potential and operational risk to support site selection?

## Objective

Create an interactive dashboard to assess site feasibility across countries and therapeutic indications, compare key metrics and identify promising site–indication combinations.

## Methods

Built with Python, Pandas, Streamlit and Plotly. The dashboard uses synthetic data generated with custom Python code. Recruitment estimates and risk categories are based on illustrative rules and assumptions.

## Scope

The project focuses on site feasibility assessment rather than the full site identification process. It explores how data can support the comparison and prioritisation of potential sites, but does not cover all stages of identifying and selecting sites for a clinical trial.

## Limitations/Disclaimer

The data do not represent real clinical trial sites or outcomes. Recruitment estimates are illustrative rather than validated predictions, and risk weights and thresholds were not derived from real-world evidence. The dashboard is intended for demonstration and exploratory analysis, not for actual site selection or operational decision-making.
